<h2>Symbol 3 Owned Private Passenger Autos Only – The Commercial Auto Insurance Designation </h2>
<p><b>In plain language:</b> Symbol 3, in "owned private passenger autos only," is a detail in a commercial auto insurance policy. It tells you that the policy only covers personal-use cars that the business owns. </p>
<p><b>Technical definition:</b> In the realm of commercial auto symbols, Symbol 3 refers to "owned private passenger autos only". It is typically featured within the declarations page of a business auto policy and indicates that physical damage coverage is specifically extended only to owned private passenger autos. This is commonly seen in commercial automobile policy symbols. </p>
<p>Making a mistake with commercial auto symbols can mean significant gaps in coverage for your agency’s clients. One common area of confusion is the implications of Symbol 3 in commercial automobile insurance. </p>

<h2>What Is Symbol 3 Owned Private Passenger Autos Only in Insurance? </h2>
<p>In the context of business auto policy, Symbol 3 Owned Private Passenger Autos Only, is a type of covered auto designation symbol. This label states that the policy will only provide physical damage coverage for owned private passenger autos. These are generally passenger vehicles that the business legally owns and uses for both business and private purposes. </p>
<p>This symbol usually appears in the declarations section of a commercial automobile policy. It helps insurers and businesses better outline the extent of their coverage. For instance, this would not cover commercial vehicles such as trucks or rented or leased vehicles, specified under different symbols like Symbol 8 for hired autos only or Symbol 10 for specifically described autos. </p>

<h2>Common Questions About Symbol 3 Owned Private Passenger Autos Only </h2>
<p aria-level="3"><b>What’s the importance of Symbol 3 in Commercial Auto Insurance?</b> </p>
<p>Symbol 3 signals the specific scope of vehicle coverage in the business auto policy. Understanding how it works contributes to the clarity of the commercial auto coverage being purchased. Its significant importance lies in its specific target– the owned and private passenger autos. The limitation can help businesses to save on premiums by not paying for unnecessary coverage. </p>
<p aria-level="3"><b>How does Symbol 3 differ from other commercial auto symbols?</b> </p>
<p>Unlike other more comprehensive commercial auto symbols, Symbol 3 Owned Private Passenger Autos Only provides coverage only for private passenger autos that the business actually owns. If the business rents, leases, or hires vehicles, or if the vehicles are not private passenger autos, Symbol 3 would not provide coverage, leaving a significant gap. </p>
<p aria-level="3"><b>What happens when Symbol 3 is misunderstood in the context of insurance?</b> </p>
<p>A misunderstanding of Symbol 3 could lead to dire consequences. If a business thinks, for example, that their rented vehicle is covered when it is not, it could lead to costly out-of-pocket expenses in case of an accident. </p>

<h2>How to Explain Symbol 3 to Clients </h2>
<p><b>Personal Lines client </b>"Think of Symbol 3 as a label on your business policy that says, 'We will help payout for damages to cars your business owns. But this only applies to cars you use just like your personal family car and nothing else." </p>
<p><b>Small Business owner </b>"Symbol 3 in your commercial auto policy specifies the kind of cars your policy covers. It only covers personal use cars that your business owns. It won't cover trucks or cars your business rents or leases, which can have other symbols." </p>
<p><b>CFO or Risk Manager </b>"Understand that with Symbol 3, the commercial auto policy covers physical damages to the company-owned private passenger autos only. This would not cover the corporate trucks or hired, borrowed, or non-owned vehicles used for haulage, private hire or other uses." </p>
