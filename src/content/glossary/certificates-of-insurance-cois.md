					<h2>What Is Certificates of Insurance in Insurance? </h2>				
		<p><b>For a Client: </b>A Certificate of Insurance (COI) is a document that shows you have an active insurance policy. It summarises your coverage, so others know you're insured.  <br /><b><br />For an Insurance Professional:</b>A Certificate of Insurance (COI), often referred to as an "acord certificate" or "acord 25 certificate", is a standardized document that provides evidence of the insurance coverage. It outlines the key details of the insurance policy, such as policy limits, coverage details, policy effective date, and policy expiration. </p>

					<h2>Common Questions About Certificates of Insurance </h2>				
		<p aria-level="3"><b>What Is a COI in Insurance?</b> </p><p>A COI, or a Certificate of Insurance, is a quick way to prove that you have an active insurance policy. It's a snapshot of your policy details at the time of issuance, essential for insurance verification and compliance management. </p><p aria-level="3"><b>What's the Difference Between a Certificate of Insurance and an Insurance Binder?</b> </p><p>The main distinction is that an insurance binder temporarily serves as proof of insurance, whereas a COI is a more formal and long-term document. </p><p aria-level="3"><b>How Can I Get a Certificate of Insurance?</b> </p><p>You can usually request a COI from your insurance company (or insurance agent). The request is then sent to the insurance carrier to issue the document. </p><p aria-level="3"><b>What Information Do Certificates of Insurance Contain?</b> </p><p>COIs contain various insurance details, including the type of coverage (such as liability insurance, property insurance, or workers compensation), the policy limits, and the policy terms. </p><p aria-level="3"><b>What is COI Tracking?</b> </p><p>COI tracking involves monitoring the status and validity of various COIs, ensuring that they are kept up-to-date and meet insurance requirements. Some businesses use COI tracking software to automate this process. </p>
