					<h2>What Is Civil in Insurance? </h2>				
		<p>From a <b>client’s point of view</b>, a civil claim is when they seek compensation from another party for an issue like an injury, property damage, or a contract dispute. It doesn't involve the police, but lawyers and potentially a court judgment. <br /></p><p>From a <b>technical insurance perspective</b>, a civil claim often arises when a liability policy triggers. This includes parts of the policy like liability limits, exclusions, deductibles, and conditions. The policy might may mention tort or legal obligation, which are other types of civil claims.</p>

					<h2>Common Questions About Civil </h2>				
		<p aria-level="3"><b>What's the difference between a civil and criminal case?</b> </p><p>A <b>civil case</b> usually involves private disputes between individuals or organizations. The person or entity initiating the case, known as the plaintiff, seeks a remedy usually in the form of monetary damages. </p><p>However, a <b>criminal case</b> involves offenses against the society, state, or public, even if it involves an individual victim. The cases are initiated by a government prosecutor. </p><p aria-level="3"><b>How does a civil lawsuit affect an insurance policy?</b> </p><p>A civil lawsuit pertains to a claim against an insured, and the liability portion of the client's insurance policy often responds to it. For instance, in an auto policy, the bodily injury liability coverage would pay up to the policy limit if the insured is held responsible for causing bodily injury in an accident, and a civil lawsuit is filed for it. </p><p aria-level="3"><b>Are all civil cases insurable?</b> </p><p>Not all civil cases are insurable. Intentional wrongful acts, for example, are generally excluded in liability policies. </p><p aria-level="3"><b>What is the agency's role in a client's civil case?</b> </p><p>An agency's role is providing the right protection for clients, educating them on potential risks, and guiding them through claims. However, they do not act as the client's lawyer or insurer. After a loss, the agency assists in facilitating the claims process with the insurer. </p>

					<h2>Real Claim Examples Involving Civil </h2>				
		<p><b>Scenario 1: </b>A client's dog bit a visitor, causing injury. The injured party filed a civil lawsuit seeking compensation for medical expenses. The client's homeowners policy covered the claim under personal liability coverage. </p><p><b>Scenario 2: </b>A client's advertising unintentionally infringed on another business's trademark. The aggrieved party filed a civil lawsuit. Fortunately, the client's commercial liability policy included personal and advertising injury coverage. <br /></p><p><b>Scenario 3: </b>A former employee sued the client for wrongful termination—a civil claim. Unfortunately, the client's general liability policy didn't cover employment practices liability—the employer liability was exposed. </p>

					<h2>How to Explain Civil to Clients </h2>				
		<p>To a <b>Personal Lines client:</b> "Civil claims relate to disputes among individuals or businesses serviced through court actions. If someone slips on your icy driveway, that's a civil claim, not a criminal offense." </p><p>To a <b>Small Business owner:</b> "Civil claims are when someone, possibly a customer or another business, takes you to court to seek financial compensation. If a customer falls and gets hurt at your location, they may file a civil claim, each handled by your business liability policy." <br /></p><p>To a <b>CFO or Risk Manager:</b> "Managing the risk of civil claims is crucial. They are lawsuits brought by individuals or businesses against your company seeking monetary damages. It's vital to have robust liability coverage to protect your firm's assets and reputation." </p>
