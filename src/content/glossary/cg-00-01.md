<h2>CG 00 01 – An ISO General Liability Coverage Form </h2>
<p><b>In plain language:</b> CG 00 01 is the standard form insurance companies use for commercial general liability (CGL) coverage. In simple terms, it's the template that sets out the basic rules for how a CGL policy works. </p>
<p><b>Technical definition:</b> CG 00 01 is the ISO Commercial General Liability (CGL) coverage form. It provides the details of key coverage features, including the insuring agreement, exclusions, conditions, policy definitions, and the policyholder's duties in the event of an incident. It is widely used in the industry and forms the backbone of most standard CGL policies. </p>
<p>Ever wondered about the backbone form of a commercial general liability policy? The answer lies within the CG 00 01 form. This foundational document can make or break a claim—making it a vital part of any conversation about liability coverage. </p>

<h2>What Is CG 00 01 in Insurance? </h2>
<p>In insurance, CG 00 01 is the commercial general liability (CGL) coverage form widely used in the United States. It is the foundational document for the CGL policy, outlining the insurer's duty to defend against claims for damages because of bodily injury, property damage, or personal and advertising injury. </p>
<p>Where it appears: CG 00 01 is the main form used in the Declarations of a CGL Policy. </p>
<p>The form also establishes the conditions under which insurance applies, explains coverage territory, and sets the general aggregate limit—the maximum amount the insurer will pay during the policy period. It does not cover new or emerging risks unless specifically stated, which is why it often varies by state and carrier. Always check the specific policy form. </p>

<h2>Common Questions About CG 00 01 </h2>
<p aria-level="3"><b>What's the significance of the CG 00 01 form in liability coverage?</b> </p>
<p>The CG 00 01 form is crucial because it sets the foundation for liability coverage. It outlines the insurer’s obligations, including duty to defend against liabilities arising from property damage or personal and advertising injuries. </p>
<p aria-level="3"><b>Does the CG 00 01 form differ for each policy?</b> </p>
<p>While the CG 00 01 form serves as a standard template, insurance companies may modify it to cater to specific situations, risks, or state regulations. Always check the specific policy form. </p>
<p aria-level="3"><b>Does the CG 00 01 form cover access to confidential information liabilities?</b> </p>
<p>Generally, CG 00 01 does not cover claims stemming from access to confidential information, unless it's caused by personal and advertising injury under the conditions provided in the policy. It's important for agents to note this and clarify with clients. </p>
<p aria-level="3"><b>Can the form change over time?</b> </p>
<p>The ISO periodically updates the CG 00 01 form, such as cg 00 01 07 98, cg 00 01 10 93, cg 00 01 04 13, cg 00 01 10 01, and iso cg 00 01 04 13. These updates reflect changes in legal interpretations and emerging risks faced by businesses. </p>
<h2>CG 00 01 vs. CG 00 </h2>
<p>The CG 00 series is a broad category encompassing all commercial general liability forms, while CG 00 01 is a specific form within this series.  </p>

<h2>Real Claim Examples Involving CG 00 01 </h2>
<p><b>Scenario 1: </b>A small marketing agency faced legal action over allegedly stolen ad ideas. Having a sound knowledge of CG 00 01, their insurance broker had highlighted the personal and advertising injury coverage within their CGL policy, which offered protection against such risks. This understanding allowed the agency to successfully claim defense costs under the policy's duty to defend clause. </p>
<p><b>Scenario 2: </b>A retail store faced a liability claim from a customer injured on its premises in a rented property overseas. But because their CGL policy's CG 00 01 form restricted the coverage territory to the U.S, they were unable to successfully claim their defense costs from the insurer. </p>
<p><b>Scenario 3: </b>A construction company faced multiple claims over a year totaling more than their general aggregate limit. Familiar with CG 00 01, their insurance broker had cautioned them about the general aggregate limit. This heads-up allowed the company to increase their coverage limit and successfully cover their claims. </p>
