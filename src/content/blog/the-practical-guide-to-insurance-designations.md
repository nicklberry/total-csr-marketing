					<h2>Breakdown of Top Insurance Designations and Certifications:</h2>				
		<p>Here is a closer look at some of the most common insurance designations:</p>		
					<h3>CISR – Certified Insurance Service Representative</h3>				
		<p>The CISR program was created by The National Alliance and is meant for individuals with roles focused on servicing the day-to-day needs of clients.  The primary goal of this series is to help those in customer service roles enhance the knowledge and skills needed to advise and build stronger relationships with clients.  </p><p><strong><a href="https://totalcsr.com/insurance-agency-blog/cisr-designation/">CISR</a> </strong>requires the completion of 5 out of 9 classes with a multiple-choice exam at the end of each.  Optional certification courses in this program include Agency Operations, Commercial Casualty I, Commercial Casualty II, Elements of Risk Management, Insuring Commercial Property, Insuring Personal Auto Exposures, and Insurance Personal Residential.  </p><p>CISR designees can also earn their CISR Elite designation by successfully completing all 9 of the courses listed above. <a href="https://www.scic.com/cisr"><b>For more information on the CISR Insurance Designation</b></a></p>		
					<h3>CIC – Certified Insurance Counselor</h3>				
		<p>The CIC coursework is offered by The National Alliance and the information presented is more in-depth and advanced than that of the CISR course.  The CIC program offers a wide variety of topics allowing agents who pursue this designation to expand their knowledge in several areas.</p><p>CIC requires the completion of 5 courses and exams in order to earn the CIC designation.  Each class includes 16 hours of instruction with a 2-hour essay exam at the conclusion of the class.  Optional courses for this designation include Agency Management, Commercial Casualty, Commercial Multiline, Commercial Property, Insurance Company Operations, Life & Health, and Personal Lines. <a href="https://www.scic.com/cic"><strong>For more information about the CIC Insurance Designation</strong></a></p>		
					<h3>CRM – Certified Risk Manager</h3>				
		<p>The CRM designation is another program offered by The National Alliance.  CRM classes are designed to help agents build expertise and become more proficient in the 5 steps of risk management.   Coursework is focused on analyzing, financing, controlling, identifying, and managing risk for clients.  </p><p>The CRM designation is earned through the completion of 5 classes and exams.   Courses include Analysis of Risk, Control of Risk, Financing of Risk, Practice of Risk Management, Principles of Risk Management.  Each class provides 16 hours of instruction with an exam at the conclusion. <a href="https://www.scic.com/crm"><strong>For additional details on the CRM Insurance Designation</strong></a></p>		
					<h3>ARM – Associate in Risk Management</h3>				
		<p>The ARM designation focuses on helping individuals develop deeper knowledge on various elements of risk management including analysis and implementation techniques, risk retention, risk transfer, and risk financing.  </p><p>In order to earn the ARM designation, individuals are required to complete 4 self-study online courses and exams.   The ARM course work covers a wide range of topics including Risk in an Evolving World, Holistically Assessing Risk, Successfully Treating Risk, and Ethics. <a href="https://web.theinstitutes.org/designations/associate-risk-management"><strong>For additional details on the ARM Insurance Designation</strong></a></p>		
					<h3>AINS – Associate in General Insurance</h3>				
		<p>The AINS designation is a professional insurance designation offered by The Institutes. The AINS courses provide an overview of general insurance information allowing attendees to build a solid foundation on basic insurance principles.</p><p>Individuals wishing to obtain an AINS designation will need to complete 4 courses and exams.  The AINS coursework starts with core learnings of Property and Liability Insurance Principles, a concentrated course on Commercial or Personal Lines, an elective, and ethics. <a href="https://web.theinstitutes.org/designations/associate-general-insurance"><strong>For additional details on the AINS Insurance Designation</strong></a></p>		
					<h3>AAI – Accredited Advisor in Insurance</h3>				
		<p>he AAI designation courses are provided by The Institutes and designed for agency employees in any role.  The information covered provides an extensive review of all elements of the Property and Casualty Insurance Industry.</p><p>The AAI designation is earned by completing 4 self-study courses and exams.  The AAI coursework provides education Foundations of Insurance Production, Multiple-Lines Insurance Production, Agency Operations, and Sales Management and Ethics. <a href="https://web.theinstitutes.org/designations/accredited-adviser-insurance"><strong>For additional details on the AAI Insurance Designation</strong></a></p>		
					<h3>CPCU – Chartered Property Casualty Underwriter</h3>				
		<p>CPCU is a professional certification designed for individuals desiring to pursue in-depth information on risk management concepts.  The complexity of information associated with this designation makes it one of the most difficult to obtain and is held by only a small percentage of industry professionals.</p><p>Earning the CPCU certification includes completion of 9 courses and 8 exams beginning with core concepts: Foundations of Risk Management and Insurance, Insurance Operations, Business Law for Insurance Professionals, and Accounting for Insurance Professionals.   Individuals pursuing this designation will also be required to complete 3 commercial or personal lines classes and an elective course on techniques, practices, or strategies in risk management and ethics. <a href="https://web.theinstitutes.org/designations/cpcu"><b><u>Click here for additional details on the CPCU program.</u></b></a></p>
